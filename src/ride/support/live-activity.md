While you ride, Acme Ride shows a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. It lets you glance at your ride without unlocking your iPhone.

## What it shows

- Elapsed time, distance, and speed.
- Your status: a pause icon when the ride is paused, a progress ring when you're riding a route, or a rider icon otherwise.

If the Live Activity hasn't updated for a while, it shows **Reconnecting…**.

The Live Activity is for viewing only. To pause or finish, open Acme Ride.

## Turn it on or off

Go to **Settings** in Acme Ride and use **Show Live Activity**. It's on by default. Changing it during a ride takes effect straight away.

If Settings says "Live Activities are turned off for Acme Ride in iOS Settings", tap **Open Settings** and turn on **Live Activities** for Acme Ride.

## Recording with the screen locked

Acme Ride keeps recording your route, sensors, and radar alerts with your iPhone locked, whether the Live Activity is on or off.
