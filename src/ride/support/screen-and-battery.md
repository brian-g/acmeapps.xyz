A bright screen and GPS are the biggest drains on your battery during a ride. Acme Ride is designed to use only what it needs.

## When the screen stays on

Your iPhone won't auto-lock while all of these are true:

- the dashboard is open,
- the ride is recording (not paused),
- Acme Ride is the app on screen.

When you minimize the dashboard, pause the ride, or switch apps, your iPhone auto-locks as normal. Recording continues either way.

## Auto-dim

With **Auto-dim** on (Settings, on by default), the screen dims when you haven't touched the dashboard for 30 seconds. Tap anywhere to bring it back to full brightness. That tap only wakes the screen, so you won't hit a button by accident.

Use the **Dimmed Brightness** slider in Settings to choose how dim it gets, from 0% to 50% (10% by default). The screen previews the level as you drag. Your usual brightness comes back when the ride ends. If your brightness is already lower than the dimmed level, Acme Ride doesn't raise it.

## Tips for long rides

- Leave **Auto-dim** on and set **Dimmed Brightness** as low as you can comfortably read.
- Minimize the dashboard or lock your iPhone on long stretches where you don't need to look at it. The Live Activity and audio alerts keep working.
- Carry a small battery pack for rides longer than your iPhone usually lasts.
- Charge your sensors before long rides. The ride summary tells you if one ran low.

GPS is used only while you're recording a ride. Acme Ride doesn't track your location in the background at other times.
