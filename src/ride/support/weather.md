Acme Ride uses Apple Weather to show conditions before and during your ride.

## Before you ride

- **New Ride sheet**: the **Weather** section shows current conditions where you are, or at the start of the route you've chosen.
- **Route details**: each route shows the weather at its start, and its map shows the forecast wind along the way.

If the sheet says "Turn on Location to see the weather here", Acme Ride doesn't have location access. See [How Acme Ride uses your location](/ride/support/location-data/). "Offline: Weather unavailable" means your iPhone has no internet connection.

## During the ride

Add the **Weather** widget to your dashboard to see the wind as you meet it, shown relative to the direction you're riding. When rain is forecast, the widget shows rain instead. Tap the widget for more detail. Acme Ride updates the weather about every 10 minutes during a ride.

## Rain ahead

When you're riding a route, Acme Ride checks the forecast along the route ahead. If there's a 50% or higher chance of rain in the next two hours where you'll be, a banner appears for a few seconds, for example "Rain 70% · 18.4 km ahead", or "Rain 70% · here" if it's where you are now.

Acme Ride checks the route ahead only while you're on the route and the dashboard is open. It stops while the ride is paused or the app is in the background.

## Privacy

To get the weather, Acme Ride sends Apple your location or points on your route. No account or identifier is attached. See [How Acme Ride uses your location](/ride/support/location-data/).
