## Start a ride

1. Tap **Start Ride** (the play icon at the top right of any tab).
2. The **New Ride** sheet opens. Check it over:
   - **Route**: choose a route to follow, or leave it at **None** for a free ride. With a route, you can also turn **Turn-by-Turn** on or off.
   - **Weather**: current conditions where you are, or at the start of your route.
   - **Sensors**: each paired sensor and whether it's **Connected** or still **Searching**.
3. Tap **Start Ride**.

The dashboard opens and recording begins. Acme Ride keeps recording with your iPhone locked or while you use another app.

## During the ride

The buttons at the bottom of the dashboard are:

- **Pause**: pauses recording. While paused, you'll see **Resume** and **Finish**.
- **Ring Bell**: plays a bicycle bell sound to warn people ahead.
- **Sensors**: appears only when a sensor is disconnected or low on battery.

Swipe down on the dashboard to minimize it. The ride keeps recording, and a bar above the tab bar shows it in progress. Tap the bar to open the dashboard again.

To change what the dashboard shows, see [Customize the dashboard](/ride/support/customize-the-dashboard/).

## Auto-pause

With **Auto-pause** on (Settings, on by default), Acme Ride pauses when you've been stopped for 10 seconds and resumes when you start moving again. That keeps traffic lights and café stops out of your moving time and average speed.

**Resume When Moving** (Settings, off by default) also resumes a ride you paused yourself, as soon as you move. Walking the bike counts as moving.

If Auto-pause is off and you stay stopped for 5 minutes, Acme Ride pauses the ride and asks whether you want to finish.

## Finish a ride

1. Tap **Pause**, then **Finish**.
2. Confirm with **Finish**.
3. The ride summary opens. It shows a map, your stats (distance, time, average speed, average cadence, and vehicles that passed you), an elevation profile, and time in heart rate zones.
4. Change the ride's **Name** if you like, then tap **Finish Ride**.

To throw the ride away instead, tap **Delete Ride** at the top left.

Acme Ride names rides for you. A ride on a route takes the route's name. Other rides are named for the time of day and shape, such as "Morning Loop" or "Evening Out and Back". If **Place Names** is on in Settings, the town you started in comes first, for example "Fargo Morning Loop".

When you finish, Acme Ride:

- saves the ride to your ride history,
- writes a GPX file of it to the Files app,
- saves it to Apple Health as a cycling workout, if you've allowed it.

See [Ride history and GPX export](/ride/support/ride-history-and-export/) and [How Acme Ride uses Apple Health data](/ride/support/apple-health/).

## If your ride is interrupted

Acme Ride saves your progress every 30 seconds. If your iPhone restarts or iOS closes the app mid-ride, open Acme Ride again. It picks the ride back up, dashboard open, with your route and navigation where you left them.

If you'd already tapped Finish but saving didn't complete, Acme Ride finishes saving the ride the next time you open it.
