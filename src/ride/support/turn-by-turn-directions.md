When you ride a route with **Turn-by-Turn** turned on, Acme Ride alerts you before each turn. To load a route, see [Load a route](/ride/support/load-a-route/).

## Turn it on or off

On the **New Ride** sheet, after you choose a route, use the **Turn-by-Turn** switch. It's on by default, and Acme Ride remembers your choice for next time. With it off, the route still appears on the map, but you won't get turn alerts.

## Turn alerts

About 100 metres (330 feet) before each turn:

- A banner shows the turn in the middle of the dashboard. It uses the cue text from your GPX file, or **Turn left**, **Turn right**, **Bear left**, **Bear right**, or **Make a U-turn**.
- A tone plays: a rising tone for a right turn and a falling tone for a left turn.

The banner stays up until you reach the turn.

If a radar warning is sounding at the same moment, the turn tone is held back so the two don't play over each other. The banner still appears.

Turn alerts come from the cues in your GPX file, so export your route with cue sheets or turn instructions turned on. Ride with GPS and Komoot exports can include them. A route with no cues still shows on the map and tells you when you're off route, but it has no turn alerts.

## Route widgets

These widgets are useful on a route. Add them with [Customize the dashboard](/ride/support/customize-the-dashboard/).

- **Directions**: your next turn.
- **Distance Remaining** and **ETA**.
- **Route Elevation**: the route's elevation profile, with your position on it.
- **Climb**: the climb you're on, or the next one.
- **Map**: your position on the route.

## Going off route

If you're more than about 50 metres (165 feet) from the route for about 5 seconds, an **Off route** banner appears. It clears once you're back on the route. Acme Ride doesn't plan a new route for you, so use the map to find your way back.

Navigation ends when you reach the end of the route. The ride keeps recording until you finish it.
