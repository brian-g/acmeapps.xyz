Every ride you finish is saved in the **Rides** tab. Rides are stored only on your iPhone.

## Review a ride

Tap a ride in the **Rides** tab to see its map, stats, elevation, heart rate, and the sensors it used.

Tap the map to open it full screen. **Map Options** lets you:

- switch between **Standard** and **Satellite** maps,
- show **Vehicle Passes** (from a radar) and **Stops**,
- **Color Route By** **Speed**, **Grade**, **Cadence**, or **Heart Rate**, or leave it **Plain**.

## Edit a ride

Open the ride and tap **Edit** to change its **Title** or add **Notes**, then tap **Save**. Renaming a ride also updates its GPX file.

## Export a ride as GPX

Acme Ride writes a GPX file for every ride you finish, so you can upload it to Strava, Garmin Connect, Ride with GPS, or any app that reads GPX.

**Share one ride**

Open the ride and tap **Share GPX**. Choose where to send it: AirDrop, Mail, Messages, Files, or another app.

**Find all your rides in Files**

Open the **Files** app and go to **On My iPhone → Acme Ride → Rides**. Each file is named with the date and time the ride started, for example `AcmeRide_2026-11-18_07-30.gpx`.

Each GPX file includes your track with speed, heart rate, and cadence at every point, plus a waypoint for each vehicle that passed you. Apps that read Garmin's track extensions, as most do, will show your heart rate and cadence.

## Delete a ride

- In the **Rides** tab, swipe left on a ride and tap **Delete**. It's deleted right away.
- From the summary after you finish, tap **Delete Ride** and confirm.

Deleting a ride also deletes its workout from Apple Health. The ride's GPX file stays in the Files app until you delete it there.

There's no way to delete all rides at once. Deleting the Acme Ride app removes all its rides and routes from your iPhone. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there.
