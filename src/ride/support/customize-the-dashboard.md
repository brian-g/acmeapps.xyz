The dashboard is the screen you see while you ride. It's made of pages of widgets. Swipe left or right to change pages. The dots at the bottom show which page you're on. You can change any page to show the numbers you care about.

You edit the dashboard during a ride. Start a ride, make your changes, and they're kept for every ride after.

## Edit the dashboard

1. Start a ride. You can pause it while you edit.
2. Touch and hold anywhere on a dashboard page. The widgets start to jiggle, as on the iPhone Home Screen.
3. Make your changes:
   - **Move a widget:** drag it to an empty space, or onto a widget of the same size to swap them. Other widgets don't shift to make room.
   - **Remove a widget:** tap its remove button.
   - **Add a widget:** tap the **Add Widget** (+) button at the top, or tap an empty space.
4. Tap **Done** (✓) at the top.

If you use VoiceOver, choose **Edit Dashboard** from the actions on the page dots. Each widget then offers **Move Up**, **Move Down**, **Move Left**, and **Move Right** actions.

## Add a widget

The **Add Widget** sheet groups widgets into **Ride**, **Heart**, **Fitness**, **Elevation**, **Weather**, and **Route**. Each widget is shown in every size it comes in. Sizes that won't fit in the space left on the page are dimmed.

Each page is a grid two columns wide and seven rows tall. Widgets come in three sizes:

- **Small**: half the width, one row.
- **Wide**: full width, one row.
- **Large**: full width, two rows.

A widget can appear only once on a page, but you can put the same widget on several pages.

## Add or remove a page

In **Add Widget**, the **Page** section has **Empty page**. It adds a blank page after the one you're on. Fill it with widgets, then tap **Done**. Pages left empty are removed when you tap **Done**.

## Available widgets

| Widget | Group | Sizes | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speed | Ride | Small, Wide, Large | Current speed |
| Speed Gauge | Ride | Large | Current speed on a dial |
| Average Speed | Ride | Small | Average moving speed |
| Duration | Ride | Small | Moving time, not counting stops and pauses |
| Distance | Ride | Small | Distance so far |
| Cadence | Ride | Small, Wide | Pedalling cadence. Needs a paired cadence sensor. |
| Pace | Ride | Small | Time per kilometre or mile |
| Sunset | Ride | Small | Time left until sunset |
| Heart Rate | Heart | Small, Wide | Current heart rate |
| HR Zones | Heart | Small, Wide | Your current heart rate zone |
| Active Calories | Fitness | Small | Calories burned |
| Ascent | Elevation | Small | Total climbing |
| Descent | Elevation | Small | Total descending |
| Grade | Elevation | Small | Current gradient |
| Elevation | Elevation | Wide | Current elevation |
| Weather | Weather | Small, Wide | Wind relative to your direction, or rain |
| Directions | Route | Small, Wide | Your next turn |
| Map | Route | Large | Your position and route, heading up |
| Route Elevation | Route | Wide | Elevation profile of the route, with your position |
| Distance Remaining | Route | Small | Distance to the end of the route |
| ETA | Route | Small | Estimated arrival time |
| Climb | Route | Wide | The climb you're on or the next one |

The Cadence widget is hidden in **Add Widget** until you pair a cadence sensor. Route widgets show information only when you're riding a route.

## Radar

The radar isn't a widget. When a paired Garmin Varia connects during a ride, a radar column appears along the right edge of every page. See [Radar alerts](/ride/support/radar-alerts/).

## More detail

Tap a widget to open more detail. For example, Heart Rate shows **Time in Zones**, and the Map opens full screen. The full-screen map has buttons to switch between heading-up and north-up, and to show the whole route.

## Going back to the original layout

There's no reset button. If you arrange a page back to how it started, Acme Ride treats it as the default layout again.

## Other display settings

- **Units** (Settings): Metric or Imperial for speed, distance, and elevation.
- **Auto-dim** (Settings): dims the screen during a ride. See [Screen, brightness, and battery](/ride/support/screen-and-battery/).
- Acme Ride follows your iPhone's Light and Dark appearance.
