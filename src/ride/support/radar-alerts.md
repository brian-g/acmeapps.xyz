With a Garmin Varia RTL515 or RCT715 paired, Acme Ride shows vehicles coming up behind you and warns you with sound and vibration. To pair a radar, see [Pair a sensor](/ride/support/pair-a-sensor/).

Acme Ride's radar alerts help you stay aware of traffic. They can't see everything, so keep watching and listening.

## The radar column

Once the radar connects during a ride, a column appears along the right edge of every dashboard page. Each approaching vehicle is a marker. It moves up the column as the vehicle closes in, up to about 140 metres behind you. The column changes colour with the alert level. It can show up to eight vehicles at a time.

## Alert levels

Acme Ride rates each moment by how fast traffic is closing on you:

| Level | When | You'll feel and hear |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | A vehicle is approaching less than 15 km/h (about 9 mph) faster than you | A light vibration, no sound |
| **2** | A vehicle is approaching 15–30 km/h (9–19 mph) faster than you, or three or more vehicles are approaching | A firmer vibration and a double beep, played once |
| **3** | A vehicle is approaching 30 km/h (19 mph) or more faster than you | A strong vibration and a repeating alarm until the danger passes |

When a level 2 or 3 alert clears, a falling **all-clear** tone plays. Acme Ride waits at least 3 seconds before repeating an alert at the same level, so a line of traffic doesn't set off a stream of alerts.

### Sound with Silent mode on

Radar tones play even when the Ring/Silent switch is set to silent and while your iPhone is locked, because they're safety alerts. Check your iPhone's volume before you ride.

## Vehicle passes

Acme Ride counts the vehicles that pass you. The count appears in the ride summary and in your ride history, and each pass is marked on the ride's map. To show them on the map, open a ride, tap the map, and turn on **Vehicle Passes** in **Map Options**. Passes are also saved as waypoints in the ride's GPX file.

## If the radar disconnects

If the radar drops out during a ride, Acme Ride waits 10 seconds for it to come back. If it doesn't, you'll feel a single light vibration and the column shows the radar as offline. Acme Ride keeps trying to reconnect for the rest of the ride.

Acme Ride shows the radar's battery level while it's connected. The ride summary tells you if the battery ran low during the ride.

## Not supported

The Garmin Varia RearVue 820 uses a secured connection that other apps can't read, so it doesn't work with Acme Ride.
