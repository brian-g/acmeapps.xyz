# About

Acme Apps makes small, focused apps that do one thing well. Each one starts with
a problem I actually have, like a bike computer I can read in full sun, or
knowing how fast the car behind me is going, and stops once that problem is
solved.

## Principles

- **Small on purpose.** No feature lists padded for a comparison chart. If a
  screen doesn't earn its place, it goes.
- **Private by default.** No accounts, no logins, no ads, no tracking. Your data
  stays on your device unless you choose to send it somewhere.
- **Pay once.** Paid apps are a fair one-time price, not a subscription. Utilities
  are free.
- **Accessible from day one.** Accessibility is part of the design, not a fix
  added later.
- **Native.** Built to feel at home on iPhone and Mac.

I'm Brian Glaeske, a retired user experience designer who specialized in
interaction design.

I spent sixteen years at Microsoft designing business software for Dynamics AX
and Dynamics 365 Talent. I was the UI architect for its web presentation
framework, wrote its accessibility and HTML/CSS guidelines, and hold a patent for
an adaptable user interface for business software. After that I spent nine years
as Director of Product and User Experience at FarmQA. I designed their apps and
website and built their design system.

Now I get to make the apps I want to use. I build them with SwiftUI and agentic
AI coding tools, which lets one designer ship what used to take a team. The
lesson I'm keeping from my career is that the best design feedback comes straight
from the people using the product.

For more of my work, visit [brian.glaeske.us](https://brian.glaeske.us).
